Is this for you?

You might recognize yourself here.

  • You left — or were pushed out of — a job that was making you smaller.
  • You keep replaying certain moments, certain things that were said to you.
  • You've started to wonder, quietly, if they were right about you.
  • You're sharp and capable, and people in your life tell you that — and you still can't quite believe it right now.
  • You know something was wrong about what happened. You just can't fully trust your own read of it anymore.
  • You're ready to do real work — not venting, not networking, not being told to believe in yourself. The actual work of untangling.

The six weeks

From wreckage to truth to power.

Phase 1: name it
Phase 2: examine it
Phase 3: reclaim it

Week 1

The wreckage

Full witness to what happened. No reframing. No silver linings. Just telling the story — out loud, without apology — and being believed.

Week 2

Gaslighting 101

Name the mechanism. Understanding how gaslighting works — neurologically, psychologically — begins to separate what happened from who you are.

Week 3

The stories we inherited

Where did this lie land so easily? We trace the older stories that made fertile ground — not as blame, but as archaeology.

Week 4

Separating self from story

The pivot. Using ACT-based defusion and narrative coaching, we practice holding the story at arm's length. You are not what happened to you.

Week 5

Evidence of you

We build the counter-narrative from actual evidence — your contributions, your impact, your character. Receipts the old story cannot argue with.

Week 6

Power forward

Write the new story from a place of truth, not performance. Wiser, boundaried, and ready for what comes next.

How this works

A container you can actually trust.

Untangling is a small, closed group. What is shared inside the room stays inside the room — full stop. Every participant agrees to complete confidentiality before the first session begins.

This is a judgment-free space. There is no right way to have experienced what you experienced, no correct way to feel about it, and no performance expected of you here. You come as you are.

You do not pay anything until you have been accepted and assigned to a cohort. The application is free. The commitment — financial and otherwise — comes only after we both know this is the right fit.
